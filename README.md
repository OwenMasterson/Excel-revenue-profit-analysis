# Excel-revenue-profit-analysis
# Executive Summary
This project analyses five years of company performance by comparing annual revenue and profit figures in Excel. The dataset tracks results from 2017 to 2021 and visualises them through a line chart to highlight overall financial trends. Both revenue and profit show consistent year‑on‑year growth, with revenue increasing at a faster rate, indicating expanding business activity and improving profitability over time. The analysis provides a simple but effective view of financial health, helping identify long‑term performance patterns and supporting data‑driven decision‑making.

# Key Insights
* Both revenue and profit increase steadily from 2017 to 2021
* Revenue grows at a faster rate than profit, indicating expanding business activity
* The upward trend suggests improving financial health and operational performance
* The dataset supports quick year‑to‑year comparison for strategic decision‑making

# Technical Skills Demonstrated

* Dataset Structure
The dataset includes:
* Year
* 12‑Month Profit
* 12‑Month Revenue
* Grand Totals for both metrics
This structure supports clear comparison across time and simplifies visualisation

# Visualisation
The line chart compares revenue and profit across five years, making it easy to:
* Identify growth patterns
* Spot divergence between revenue and profit
* Understand long‑term financial direction
